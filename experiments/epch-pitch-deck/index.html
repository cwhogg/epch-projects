Process and timelines for implementation

We typically get customers live in ~4 weeks, and then do weekly checkins until we are all happy with the implementation. Following the implentation phase, we will have QBRs with recommended improvements every 3 months.

1Data Collection
  • Interviews with key Leadership Team
  • 2 hour workshop with Leadership Team
  • Document collection
  • Targeted survey to LT, managers or whole company (on use cases)
2MCP Server Development
  • EPCH process all information from interviews, surveys and documents
  • All data processed under BAA (if necessary)
  • Review foundation documents with LT (KB structure, mission, vision, voice, principles)
3Soft Launch
  • Deploy MCP server to Leadership Team Claude instances
  • Frequent checkins and feedback
  • 2 hour workshop post trial period with pros/cons and updates desired
4Company Launch
  • EPCH will enable the MCP server to all employees
  • EPCH will conduct training sessions (as desired)
  • EPCH will distribute documentation and video support for use
  • EPCH will deploy a 24/7 customer support rep for [company] (AI of course)
